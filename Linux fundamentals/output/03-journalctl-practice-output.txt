=============== 9. Housekeeping (not run here — they delete logs) ===============
  sudo journalctl --vacuum-size=200M   # trim the journal down to 200 MB
  sudo journalctl --vacuum-time=7d     # drop anything older than a week
  sudo journalctl --verify             # integrity check
